## Ratios that moved >5% vs 2026-11-12 (confirmed by 2 extra rounds)

| benchmark | prev | v2 | rerun1 | rerun2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| binary_trees | 1.11 | 1.17 | 1.21 | 1.22 |
| chaos | 0.99 | 0.85 | 0.86 | 0.85 |
| float | 1.22 | 1.27 | 1.13 | 1.22 |
| set_partition | 1.01 | 0.77 | 0.80 | 0.79 |
| np_arc_distance | - | 1.32 | 1.32 | 1.34 |

Typhon absolute times are essentially unchanged for chaos (0.70-0.80 s) while
Codon got slower under load (0.71 -> 0.94 s), so chaos is a Codon-side swing.
set_partition improved on the Typhon side (0.96 -> 0.82 s), consistent with the
static runtime (no PLT hop into libtyphonrt) and the bdwgc growth policy.
binary_trees is ~5% slower in Typhon (0.33 -> 0.35 s): allocation-heavy, likely
the GC_do_blocking/no-signal-retry and LARGE_CONFIG changes; peak RSS dropped
from 57 to 30 MiB at the same time (smaller heap, more collections).
np_arc_distance: elementwise ufunc chain on 10M arrays (sin/cos/arcsin/sqrt);
not BLAS, so the Accelerate switch does not help. Follow-up for the numpy lane.

## Root cause of the max RSS above Codon (v4)
`TYPHON_GC_STATS=2` traces every post-growth stop-func decision. In the high-RSS np_matmul runs the
post-growth veto (meant for small-object programs, where bdwgc 8.3 forces a GC after every heap growth)
was approving consecutive 32 MB array requests without a collection. The heap then grew by 2 to 3 dead
arrays (heap 229 to 262 MB vs 164 MB, RSS 255 to 289 MB). How many vetoes chained depended on when
`bytes_allocd_since_gc` crossed `in_use/div`, which explains the bimodal result.
Fix: requests of 1 MiB or more never veto (thread-local flag set in `seq_alloc`/`seq_alloc_atomic`).
np_matmul max RSS 289 -> 211 MB. float/binary_trees keep the veto (an absolute cap of 0 to 1 MB made
float 3x slower). Allocation flags already match the C++ runtime (same GC_* setup, atomic arrays).

## eigh 128 at 4 BLAS threads
The bench binary does not link OpenMP. Threads only reach Accelerate. Ten paired interleaved runs with
OMP_NUM_THREADS=VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=4 gave a median of 3.38 s for both, so the earlier +12% was noise.

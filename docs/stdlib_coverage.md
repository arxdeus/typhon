## pystd-final additions
- datetime: strftime/strptime (common directives), `__format__`, 3.14 fractional-second fromisoformat. Still missing: timezone/tzinfo, astimezone, %z/%Z.
- calendar: TextCalendar, prmonth, calendar(), weekheader.
- string: Template (substitute, safe_substitute, is_valid, get_identifiers).
- ipaddress: IPv6Address, IPv6Network (no ip_address/ip_network v6 dispatch yet).
- Verified equal to CPython: json dumps/loads typed paths, textwrap, bisect key=, heapq key=, statistics ranked correlation and proportional regression, decimal sqrt/ln/exp/log10.
- Gaps: Decimal ** Decimal, untyped heterogeneous json.dumps (Optional[int] vs Optional[bool] list type error).
